Eighth Point Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-20

Eighth Point is an independent companion app for the Riftbound trading card game, made by one developer. It is unofficial: not made, endorsed or sponsored by Riot Games. This policy says what the app keeps, where it goes, and how to delete it.

The short version: your matches, collection and settings are yours. They sync to your own account so they follow you to a new phone. Nobody else can read them, they are never combined with other players' data or published, and they are never sold. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app keeps

Your account. You sign in with Apple or Google. Eighth Point never sees a password. From the sign-in, our backend keeps:

  • your email address (if you chose Apple's "Hide My Email", the private relay address Apple gives us);
  • your name, as Apple or Google provides it (Apple shares it only on your first sign-in), and, from Google, a link to your profile picture, which the app does not show;
  • an account ID, and which of Apple and Google you have signed in with.

What you enter in the app. Saved on your phone first and copied to your account:

  • Matches: date, the Legends and battlefields played, scores and game-by-game results, who went first, format, duration, and anything you add: an opponent's name, an event name, link, round, type and venue, and a note.
  • Collection: the cards you add, with printing, finish, condition, language and quantity.
  • Profile: the display name you choose, your Riot game name if you add it, and your favorite Legends.

An opponent's name is something you type about another player. It stays in your account only: no other user, including that opponent, can see it, and it is never collected into anything shared.

Kept only on your phone, never sent: the round timer, your round-length settings, and a recovery copy of a match in progress.

What the app does not collect: location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health or payment information, advertising identifiers, browsing history, usage analytics or crash reports.

Where it goes and why

  • Supabase stores your account and synced data on servers in the United States, so your data survives a lost phone and appears on each phone you sign in on. Every row belongs to one account, and the database itself refuses to show it to any other account. Supabase also records the IP address and device type of each sign-in, as part of keeping sessions secure.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in. They tell us who you are; the app sends them nothing back.
  • Card images load from a third-party image host (Piltover Archive). Like any website, it sees your IP address and which card images your phone requests. It receives nothing about your account.

That is the full list. Your data is used only to run the app for you: sign-in, sync, and showing you your own matches, statistics and collection. It is not sold, rented or shared for advertising, and it is never combined with other players' data. There is no AI feature that reads your data.

Signing out and signed-out play

Signing out leaves this phone's copy of your data on the phone. A new match or card saved while signed out stays on the phone and is uploaded only if you edit it after signing in; a signed-out change to something already in your account goes to that account when you sign back in. The app assumes one person per phone; use account deletion, or delete the app, to clear a phone you hand on.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything synced to it: matches, collection, profile, favorites, and the link to your Apple or Google sign-in. There is no recovery period. The app then clears its copy of your data from that phone. Other phones signed in to the account lose access when their sign-in next refreshes; delete the app there to remove their copy.

Removing Eighth Point from your Apple or Google account settings stops that way of signing in but does not delete your data; use Delete account for that.

If you cannot use the in-app option, write to the address below and the account will be deleted.

Security

Sign-in tokens are stored in the phone's secure Keychain (Keystore on Android). Data travels over encrypted connections. Access rules in the database, not just the app, keep each account's rows private to that account.

Children

Eighth Point is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, write to the address below and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it.
