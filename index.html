Eighth Point

A companion for in-person play. Keep score at the table, save the match in seconds, and see how your Legends really perform.

Free, nothing locked. No ads, no subscription, no purchases.

01 / 04

Play

One plus and one minus each. A round clock beside the board, and every match saved on your phone first, store wifi or none.

02 / 04

Scan

Point the camera at a card, or add it by hand. Each copy keeps its printing, condition, finish and language.

03 / 04

Trade

What you give, what you get. Both totals and the difference at Low or Market prices, at the table.

04 / 04

Stats

Your record and win rate by Legend, by matchup, by opponent and by battlefield, on the play and on the draw.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

  • Private to your accountYour matches and collection are yours alone, synced across your own devices.
  • Never publishedNothing is shared with other users or published.
  • No ads, no trackingAnd no purchases. Nothing is locked.
  • Yours to take or deleteExport your matches as CSV or JSON, or delete your account from Profile, any time.
